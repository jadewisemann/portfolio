# Golden Slice

The golden slice contains the hero, the hero-to-project transition, and one representative project scene. Build only approved direction and motion decisions.

Before expansion, render and inspect desktop and mobile states. Score visual impact and art direction at least 9; motion coherence at least 9; typography and originality at least 8.5; narrative clarity and mobile at least 8. Record the result in `docs/portfolio/SCORECARD.md`.

Patch failures. After three polishing attempts on the same structure, report a structural branch rather than accumulating cosmetic fixes.
