# Final Experience Audit

Completion requires a rendered full-site review, mobile inspection, performance and accessibility audit, and regression check. Compare the final result against the locked art direction, design system, motion language, and scene graph.

Write the evidence, open risks, and final status to `docs/portfolio/FINAL_AUDIT.md`. Do not declare completion from a successful build alone.
